Privacy Policy for Syncaura Calendar

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Syncaura Calendar. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your data when you use our application and integrate it with Google services.

2. Data Collection and Google Account Access

When you use Syncaura Calendar, we request access to certain information from your Google account. We only ask for the permissions strictly necessary to provide our calendar integration features.

  • Google Calendar Data: We request read and write access to your Google Calendar to synchronize events, create new appointments, and update existing ones across our platform.
  • Basic Profile Information: We may access your name and email address to identify you within our application.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we access from your Google account is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To display your calendar events within the Syncaura application.
  • To allow you to manage (create, edit, delete) calendar events directly from our interface.
  • To synchronize changes made in Syncaura with your Google Calendar.

We do not use your Google data for targeted advertising, nor do we sell it to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • Storage: Authentication tokens provided by Google are stored securely in our database using industry-standard encryption. We do not store your Google password. [Note: Database specifics to be confirmed by the application administrators]
  • Security: All data transmitted between your browser, our servers, and Google's APIs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your Google user data with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • With trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting providers) strictly for the purpose of operating our application. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements. [Note: Specific third-party services to be confirmed]
  • When required by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention: We retain your Google data (such as sync tokens) only for as long as you maintain an active account with Syncaura Calendar.
  • Deletion: You can revoke Syncaura Calendar's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account settings. If you delete your Syncaura account, we will delete all associated Google authentication tokens and synced calendar data from our servers.
